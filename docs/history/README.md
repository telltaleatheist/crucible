# History

These are the phase plans, proposals and audits Crucible was built from. They are kept
for the reasoning and the measurements they record, and they are **not maintained**: many
of their names, routes, numbers and rulings have since changed, and nothing checks them
against the code. What must stay true is in `docs/internals/*.md`, the wire is
`docs/API.md` (generated from the app), and the product contract is `docs/INTENT.md`.
Where a document here disagrees with those, the document here is out of date.
